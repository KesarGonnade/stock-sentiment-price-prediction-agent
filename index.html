Methodology and Limitations

  • Social posts and financial news were collected from multiple sources.
  • Sentiment was classified into positive, negative and neutral categories.
  • Market features include daily returns, moving averages and volatility.
  • Prediction target is the next available trading session's closing return.
  • Support and resistance are descriptive 20-session low/high levels, not guaranteed price barriers.
  • Sentiment and market observations cover a limited period and uneven number of records.
  • Market data ends on 10 November 2026 in the collected files.
  • Sentiment session assignment uses assumed exchange cutoffs; exact source availability times and official exchange calendars were not independently verified.
  • Historical model performance does not establish future profitability or reliability.
